Anne Springs Close Greenway is located in Fort Mill, SC (just 20 minutes south of Charlotte) and features flowy, machined singletrack mostly featuring Carolina red clay that is very hardpacked and fast when it's dry. This is a great place to bring beginners as there aren't many technical features or much in the way of climbing. There's also a MTB Skills Park for beginners and intermediate riders to safely try out some features such as drops and jumps.


Green and Blue Wooden Table Top Jumps at the MTB Skills Park

Spanning 2,100 acres, the Greenway serves up roughly 12 to 14 miles of dedicated mountain bike singletrack woven into its broader 40-mile multi-use trail network. It’s the kind of place where an intermediate rider can absolutely fly, a beginner can build immense confidence, and an expert can get a killer cardio workout by just hammering the pedals.

The cost to enter can be steep for non-members, but if you have your family with you, there is plenty to eat, drink, and explore around the facility. 

MTB Skills Park


Rollers on the Green Line at the MTB Skills Park

If you want to work on leveling up your jumping, drop, or cornering skill, the Mountain Bike Skills Park offers progressive, feature-focused lines to help you ease your way into more challenging MTB trails:

  • Beginner-friendly flow lines to practice pumping.

  • Progressive drops and dirt jump lines (Green - Black).

  • Advanced wooden features, rock gardens, and skinnies

Prairie Loop

Wide, highly scenic, and fast. It takes you through open meadows and pine stands. Perfect for a warm-up or a casual cruise, but hold on tight because it lets you carry massive speed.

Paradise Loop

A local favorite. Tighter tree gaps, quicker switchbacks, and a series of fun rollers. It hugs the creek contours and feels like a true pump-track style singletrack.

Tunnel Trail

Features literal tunnels passing under local roadways, connecting different pockets of the woods. It's a blast to ride and a unique architectural quirk of the trail system.

Mill Pond

2-way trail featuring some techy climbing, rock gardens, and quick descents.
